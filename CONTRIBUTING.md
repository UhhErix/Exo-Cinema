# Contributing to Exo Cinema

Thanks for helping. Bug reports, ideas and clearer wording are welcome.

## Before you start

- **Report a problem or suggest a feature** at [getexo.app/help](https://getexo.app/help). Leave out your server's address, your user name and anything else personal.
- **Security problems** go through the same form, marked as a security problem; they are read privately.

## What you agree to

By contributing, you agree to license your contribution under the [MIT license](LICENSE). Send only work you have the right to share.

## Pull requests

Pull requests for the app and its documentation are welcome.

- Keep one change per pull request, and say what it fixes and how you checked it.
- Match the code around it: its naming, its comments and Exo's design (Exo Launcher's look is the standard).
- Words in the app are plain and short, in Exo's voice. No hint lines under controls.
